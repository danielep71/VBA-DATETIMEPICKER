# 🧭 Code of Conduct

<p align="left">
  <img alt="Scope" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/applies_to-everyone-217346">
  <img alt="Spaces" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/spaces-issues_PRs_discussions-blue">
  <img alt="Enforcement" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/enforcement-maintainer-6f42c1">
</p>

This project is a small, focused open-source effort. The goal of this Code of
Conduct is simple: keep every interaction around the Date / Time Picker
respectful, technical, and welcoming, so that people feel comfortable reporting
bugs, proposing changes, and asking questions.

---

## 🤝 Our pledge

<p align="left">
  <img alt="Pledge" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/pledge-respect_and_inclusion-217346">
</p>

Everyone who participates — by opening an issue, submitting a pull request,
commenting, or contributing in any other way — is expected to help make this a
harassment-free experience for all, regardless of experience level, background,
identity, or personal characteristics.

---

## ✅ Expected behavior

<p align="left">
  <img alt="Standards" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/standards-be_constructive-blue">
</p>

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep discussion focused on the code, the docs, and the problem at hand.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect that the maintainer may adopt, adapt, or decline a contribution to
  keep the codebase coherent.
- Help newcomers; everyone was new to VBA once.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable behavior

<p align="left">
  <img alt="Prohibited" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/prohibited-harassment-red">
</p>

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Discriminatory, demeaning, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith argument.

---

## 🛠️ Scope

<p align="left">
  <img alt="Where" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/where-project_spaces-217346">
</p>

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions, the wiki, and release threads — and to any situation
where someone is representing the project in public.

---

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

<p align="left">
  <img alt="Action" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/action-proportionate-6f42c1">
</p>

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct
and may take any action deemed appropriate, including:

- a private warning,
- editing or removing offending comments, commits, or other contributions,
- temporary or permanent blocking from project participation.

Decisions are made in good faith and aim to be proportionate to the behavior.

---

## 🙏 A note

This Code of Conduct is intentionally lightweight for a solo-maintained project.
If you prefer a widely recognized standard instead, the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) can be adopted
directly through GitHub's community-standards tooling.
